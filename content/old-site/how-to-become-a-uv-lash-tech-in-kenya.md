## What Does a UV Lash Tech Do?

A UV lash technician applies lash extensions using a UV adhesive and a UV curing lamp. Instead of waiting for glue to dry in the air, you place each extension and set it in seconds with the light.

The core lash skills are the same as classic lashing:

* Client consultation and checking for eye conditions or allergies
* Lash mapping and styling for different eye shapes
* Isolation and clean placement on each natural lash
* Hygiene and safe practice

What is new is the **UV adhesive**, the **lamp technique** and your **timing**: how much adhesive to use, how to cure without the lash shifting, and how to keep your client’s eyes protected throughout.

## Is It Hard to Be a UV Lash Tech?

Honestly? Not if you already know classic lashing.

UV lashing uses the skills you already have. The learning curve is mostly about:

* **Working with UV adhesive:** it stays workable until you cure it, which many techs actually find easier than racing fast-drying glue.
* **Curing technique:** holding and moving the lamp correctly, and curing in the right sections.
* **Safety:** keeping the client’s eyes closed and covered, and aiming the light only at the lash line.

Like any lash skill, UV takes practice more than talent. That is why our training includes live model practice and continuous mentorship after the course, so you are never figuring it out alone.

## Safety Comes First

A good UV lash tech knows when **not** to lash. In our training you will learn to check every client for eye infections, irritated eyelids, adhesive allergies, recent eye surgery and light sensitivity, and to offer a patch test when a client is unsure. Protecting your clients’ eyes is what builds a lash business that lasts.
