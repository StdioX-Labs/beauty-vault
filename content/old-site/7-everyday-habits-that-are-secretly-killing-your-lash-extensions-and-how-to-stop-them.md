# 7 Everyday Habits That Are Secretly Killing Your Lash Extensions (And How to Stop Them)

So you’ve just dropped good money and time on a fresh set of lash extensions. You’re loving the way they make your eyes pop, your makeup routine shorter, and your selfies effortlessly fierce. But within days (or maybe a week), you’re noticing lash fallout, clumping, or even bald gaps along your lash line.

**Sound familiar?** You’re not alone.

Here’s the thing most lash lovers don’t realize: **your daily habits are probably sabotaging your extensions** without you even knowing it. In this guide, we’ll cover the subtle yet destructive habits that could be causing your lashes to shed faster than they should—and what you can do instead.

According to the *American Lash Association*, 64% of premature lash extension loss is due to improper aftercare—not bad application. And a 2023 Lash Consumer Report revealed that 72% of clients admitted to rubbing their eyes at least once daily. Let’s fix that.

## You Keep Rubbing Your Eyes (Yes, Even When You’re Not Aware)

We all do it. You’re tired. Allergies are acting up. Your contact lens is being weird. Maybe your eyes just itch. But constant rubbing is the number one silent killer of lash extensions.

### Why It’s a Problem:

Rubbing your eyes loosens the adhesive that keeps your extensions attached to your natural lashes. It also causes unnecessary tension that can lead to lash breakage or total fallout.

### How to Fix It:

* **Be mindful**: Most rubbing is subconscious. Catch yourself in the act.
* **Use eye drops** for dryness or irritation.
* **Cold compresses** help soothe inflammation and itchiness.

*Pro Tip: Keep a mini hand mirror at your desk. You’re less likely to rub when you see what it’s doing to your lashes.*

## You’re Using Oil-Based Skincare or Makeup Removers

Let’s talk ingredients. Your night cream, SPF, makeup remover—even your cleanser—could be full of oils that **dissolve lash adhesive** faster than you can say “fill appointment.”

### Hidden Offenders:

* Cleansing balms
* Micellar waters (check labels!)
* Eye creams
* Face oils or oily serums

### What to Use Instead:

* **Oil-free foaming lash cleansers** (they’re made just for extensions)
* **Oil-free makeup removers**, especially around the eye area

*Pro Tip: Apply eye cream with a cotton swab, keeping a safe buffer zone around your lash line.*

*Industry Stat: 83% of lash technicians say oil exposure is the top reason for early lash shedding among their clients.*

## You Skip Cleaning Your Lashes Entirely

Yes, you actually *should* be washing your lashes. A clean lash line = better retention, fewer infections, and fluffier lashes.

### Why Cleaning is Critical:

* Natural oils, sweat, makeup, and debris build up over time.
* Dirty lashes are a **breeding ground for bacteria** (gross).
* Build-up breaks down adhesive and can cause lash clumping.

### Cleanse Like a Pro:

* Use a **foaming lash cleanser** 3–4 times a week.
* Clean with a **soft brush** and rinse gently.
* Pat dry with a lint-free towel—never rub.

*Industry Stat: Clients who cleanse regularly report 30–50% better lash retention.*

## You’re Using Regular Mascara or Eyeliner

If your lash tech gasped when you said you used mascara, now you know why.

### Why It’s Risky:

* Most mascaras (especially waterproof ones) are hard to remove and contain oils or waxes.
* Pencil and gel liners tug at the lash line.
* Removing these products often leads to rubbing or harsh chemicals.

### Better Alternatives:

* **Lash extension-safe mascara** (if you really must)
* Use **powder eyeshadow** for a soft, smokey effect
* Choose **water-based, non-oily liners** that wash off easily

*Pro Tip: Focus on upper lids only—mascara on bottom lashes can smudge into your extensions*

## You Never Brush Your Lashes

Brushing your lashes keeps them untangled, neat, and looking full. It also prevents twist-outs, where lashes go in all different directions.

### How to Brush Properly:

* Use a clean spoolie brush.
* Brush in an upward motion.
* Do it **at least once in the morning and at night**.

This small step can help you spot tangles early and prevent the kind of lash loss that comes from neglect.

*Industry Stat: Lash techs say brushing extends the wearable life of lashes by 15–20%.*

---

## You’re Overexposing Your Lashes to Heat and Steam

Lash glue (cyanoacrylate) doesn’t play well with heat or humidity. That means steamy showers, saunas, hot yoga, and even baking too close to the oven can weaken your lash bonds.

### Common Heat Offenders:

* Blow dryers on high heat settings
* Saunas and steam rooms
* Hot showers (more than 5 mins directly on the face)
* Baking, grilling, or standing over heat

### How to Protect Your Lashes:

* Keep your face away from direct steam or heat sources
* Lower your shower temp slightly
* Wear protective sunglasses when in harsh sun

*Industry Stat: Lashes exposed to consistent heat lose adhesion up to 2x faster.*

## Bonus Habit: Waiting Too Long Between Fills

While this isn’t “daily,” it’s one of the most overlooked.

Extensions shed with your natural lash cycle. Waiting too long between fills means gaps, uneven volume, and possibly a full re-do.

### Ideal Fill Schedule:

* Every **2–3 weeks** for best retention and appearance
* Letting it go longer may mean starting from scratch (which = $$)

*Pro Tip: Prebook your fills before you leave your appointment to lock in a spot.*
