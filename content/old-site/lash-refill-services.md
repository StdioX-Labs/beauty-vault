# Lash Refill Services

Lash extensions require routine maintenance to preserve their fullness, shape, and overall appeal. At Lashes by Shazz, we provide professional refill services designed to replace any shed extensions and keep your lashes looking flawless between appointments.

Refills are recommended **within 21 days** of your initial application to ensure your lashes remain voluminous and well-balanced. Refills beyond this period will be considered a **new full set**, as a significant number of extensions may have shed naturally.
