## Important Aftercare Steps

#### 1. Avoid Oil-Based Products

Oil breaks down the lash adhesive. Use oil-free cleansers and makeup removers to extend the life of your lashes.

#### 2. Do Not Rub Your Eyes

Rubbing causes premature lash shedding. If your eyes feel itchy or irritated, use a lash-safe mist or pat gently around the area.

#### 3. Brush Your Lashes Regularly

Use a clean spoolie to brush your lashes daily. This keeps them neat, separated, and free of debris.

#### 4. Schedule Timely Refills

Maintain a full, flawless look by booking refill appointments every 2–3 weeks. This keeps your lashes looking fresh and prevents uneven shedding.
