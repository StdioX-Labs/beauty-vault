## UV Lashes FAQs

#### What are UV lashes?

UV lashes are lash extensions applied with a special adhesive that sets in seconds under a gentle UV light, instead of air-drying slowly like regular lash glue. Each extension bonds firmly from the start, with no lingering fumes. At Lashes by Shazz, the #1 UV lash parlour and trainers in Kenya, UV lashes are our lead service in Nairobi. Read our [complete guide to UV lashes in Kenya](/uv-lashes-in-kenya-complete-guide).

#### Are UV lashes safe? Are UV lashes safe for your eyes?

Yes, when they are done by a trained UV lash technician. Your eyes stay closed and covered throughout, and the light is aimed at the lash line for a few seconds at a time, never into your open eyes. Because the adhesive cures instantly, there are no glue fumes to sting your eyes during the appointment. At Lashes by Shazz, every UV lash technician is trained in safe UV application.

#### Are UV lash extensions safe for everyone?

Most people can wear UV lash extensions safely, but UV adhesive is still a lash adhesive, and a small number of people react to it. If you have sensitive eyes or have reacted to lash glue before, ask Lashes by Shazz for a patch test 24 to 48 hours before your full set. If you ever notice swelling, burning or redness that does not settle, have the lashes removed and see a doctor.

#### Who can wear UV lashes, and who can’t?

UV lashes suit anyone who wants fuller lashes with less fuss, especially people with watery or sensitive eyes, busy professionals, brides and anyone who wants their set to last. Please wait before booking if you have an eye infection (such as conjunctivitis or a stye), blepharitis, a known allergy to lash adhesive, recent eye surgery, or a condition that makes your skin or eyes sensitive to light. If you are pregnant, on medication or unsure, check with your doctor first. Our Lashes by Shazz team will always ask before we start.

## Lash Extension FAQs

#### How long do lash extensions last?

Lash extensions typically last 4 to 6 weeks, depending on your natural lash cycle and aftercare. We recommend a refill every 2–3 weeks to maintain a full, fresh look.

#### Are lash extensions safe for my natural lashes?

Yes. When applied professionally and maintained properly, lash extensions do not damage your natural lashes. Our technicians use lightweight, high-quality materials and follow best practices for safe application.

#### Can I wear makeup with lash extensions?

Yes, but avoid oil-based products and waterproof mascara, as these can weaken the lash adhesive. Use lash extension–safe cleansers and makeup removers.

#### What’s the difference between Classic, Hybrid, Volume, and Mega Volume lashes?

Classic: One extension per natural lash – simple and elegant
Hybrid: A mix of classic and volume fans for added texture
Volume: Multiple ultra-light lashes per natural lash for fullness
Mega Volume: Dense fans (6D–10D) for maximum drama

#### How soon should I book a refill?

For best results, book a refill within 14–21 days after your initial appointment. After 21 days, a full set may be required.

#### Can I get my lashes wet?

Yes, but wait 24–48 hours after application to allow the adhesive to fully cure. After that, your lashes are water-resistant—just avoid excessive steam or rubbing. With UV lashes, the adhesive is cured before you leave, so there is no waiting period.
