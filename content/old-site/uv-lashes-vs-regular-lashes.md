## What Is the Difference Between UV Lashes and Regular Lashes?

The lashes themselves are the same. You can choose classic, hybrid, volume or mega volume with either method. The difference is the **adhesive** and how it sets.

* **Regular lash extensions** use an adhesive that dries in the air. It sets on the surface within minutes, but it keeps curing (and giving off fumes) for 24 to 48 hours.
* **UV lash extensions** use an adhesive that cures under a gentle UV light. Your technician places each lash and sets it with the lamp in seconds. It is fully cured before you leave.

That one change affects comfort, aftercare and how long your lashes last.

## Are UV Lashes Better Than Regular Lashes?

For most clients, yes. Here is why.

### 1. They are more comfortable

Most of the stinging people feel with lash extensions comes from glue fumes. With UV lashes, the adhesive cures instantly, so there is nothing lingering around your eyes. If your eyes water every time you get lashes done, UV lashes are worth trying.

### 2. Aftercare is simpler

With regular lashes, you have to keep them dry for the first 24 to 48 hours. With UV lashes, they are fully set when you walk out. Gym, shower, rain, no problem.

### 3. They hold better

A fully cured bond is a stronger bond. Our clients typically keep a full UV set for 4 to 6 weeks, with fewer lashes dropping in the first few days.

### When regular lashes still make sense

Regular lashes are a good choice if you are on a tighter budget, if you are trying lash extensions for the first time for one event, or if you simply prefer them. Done well by a trained technician, regular lashes are safe and beautiful too.

---

## Do UV Lashes Actually Last Longer?

Yes, and here is why it happens.

Regular adhesive is still curing after you leave the salon. Steam, water, sweat or touching your lashes in that window can weaken the bond, which is why many people lose lashes in the first week.

UV adhesive is fully cured before you leave. Nothing can disturb the bond while it sets, so your lashes hold from day one.

What UV lashes cannot do is stop your natural lash cycle. Each extension falls out with the natural lash it is attached to, usually over 4 to 6 weeks. That is why we still recommend a **refill every 2 to 3 weeks** to keep your set full.

## Are UV Lashes Safe? Are Regular Lashes Safe?

Both are safe when done by a trained technician in a clean studio. With UV lashes, your eyes stay closed under gel pads, and the light is aimed at the lash line for a few seconds at a time.

With either type, be honest about your eyes:

* Don’t book lashes if you have an eye infection, a stye or very irritated eyelids.
* Tell your technician about allergies, recent eye surgery or medication that makes you sensitive to light.
* If you have reacted to lash glue before, ask for a **patch test** 24 to 48 hours before your appointment.

## What Is the Healthiest Option for Fake Lashes?

The healthiest option is the one that is applied carefully and suits your eyes.

* **Professional lash extensions** (UV or regular), applied with the right length and weight, do not damage your natural lashes.
* **UV lashes** add comfort, because there are no glue fumes near your eyes during the appointment.
* **Daily strip lashes** put glue on your eyelid every day, and removing them can pull at the skin.
* **DIY clusters** are often glued to several natural lashes at once, which can pull them out.

If your eyes are very sensitive, start with a patch test and a light classic set.
