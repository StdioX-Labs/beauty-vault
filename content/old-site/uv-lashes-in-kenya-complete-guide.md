## What Are UV Lashes?

UV lashes are lash extensions applied with a special adhesive that cures (sets) under a gentle UV light.

With regular lash extensions, the glue dries slowly in the air. It keeps releasing fumes while it sets, and it can take 24 to 48 hours to cure fully. With UV lashes, your technician places each extension and then passes a small UV lamp over the lash line. The adhesive sets in seconds.

You get the same lash styles you already know:

* **Classic:** one extension per natural lash, for a soft, mascara-like look
* **Hybrid:** a mix of classic lashes and light volume fans
* **Volume:** several ultra-light lashes per natural lash, for a full, fluffy finish
* **Mega Volume:** dense fans for the darkest, most dramatic lash line

The difference is in how they are bonded, and that changes your whole experience.

## The Benefits of UV Lash Extensions

### 1. Instant curing

The adhesive sets in seconds under the UV light, so every lash bonds firmly from the start. Nothing is left “still drying” when you walk out of the studio.

### 2. No fumes, less irritation

Most of the stinging people feel with regular lashes comes from glue vapour while the adhesive cures. UV adhesive cures instantly, so there are no lingering fumes around your eyes. Clients with watery or sensitive eyes notice the difference straight away.

### 3. The simplest aftercare

Your UV lashes are fully set when you leave. There is no 24 to 48 hour “don’t get them wet” rule. You can wash your face, go to the gym or step into the Nairobi rain the same day.

### 4. Better retention: 4 to 6 weeks

A stronger, fully cured bond means your set stays fuller for longer between refills. Most of our clients keep a full UV set for 4 to 6 weeks with good aftercare.

## Are UV Lashes Safe?

This is the question we hear most, so here is the honest answer.

**Yes, UV lashes are safe when they are done by a trained UV lash technician.** During your appointment:

* Your eyes stay closed the whole time.
* Your lower lashes are covered with gel pads.
* The light is aimed at the lash line for a few seconds at a time, never into open eyes.

### Are UV lashes safe for your eyes?

The UV lamps used for lashes are low-power and used for seconds, with your eyes closed. The bigger safety factors are the same as with any lash extension: hygiene, the skill of your technician, and choosing the right length and weight for your natural lashes. That is where a trained lash studio makes the difference.

### Can you be allergic to UV lashes?

Yes, it is possible. UV adhesive is still a lash adhesive, and a small number of people react to it. If you have sensitive eyes or have reacted to lash glue before:

* Ask for a **patch test** 24 to 48 hours before your full set.
* Tell your technician about any allergies, eye conditions or medication.
* If you notice swelling, burning or redness that does not settle, have the lashes removed professionally and see a doctor.

No lash studio should promise that a product is “100% allergy-free”. We would rather be honest with you.

## Who Can Wear UV Lashes, and Who Can’t?

### UV lashes are great for:

* People with watery or sensitive eyes who struggle with glue fumes
* Busy professionals who want to wake up ready
* Brides, bridesmaids and anyone with a big event coming up
* Gym-goers and swimmers who need lashes that set immediately
* Anyone tired of sets that fall off in the first week

### Please wait before booking UV lashes if you have:

* An eye infection, such as conjunctivitis (pink eye) or a stye
* Blepharitis or very irritated eyelids
* A known allergy to lash adhesive
* Had recent eye surgery (wait until your doctor clears you)
* A condition or medication that makes your skin or eyes sensitive to light

If you are pregnant, undergoing medical treatment or simply unsure, check with your doctor first. Our team will always ask before we start.

## How Long Do UV Lash Extensions Last?

A UV set usually stays full for **4 to 6 weeks**. Your natural lashes still shed on their own cycle, and each extension falls out with the natural lash it is attached to. That is normal and does not mean the bond failed.

To keep them looking full, we recommend a **refill every 2 to 3 weeks**.

### How to make your UV lashes last longer

1. Avoid oil-based cleansers, makeup removers and serums near your eyes.
2. Skip waterproof mascara. Your lashes don’t need it.
3. Don’t rub or pull at your lashes, especially when you wake up.
4. Brush them gently with a clean spoolie every morning.
5. Wash your lashes regularly with a lash-safe cleanser to keep them clean.
6. Try to sleep on your back, or use a silk pillowcase.
7. Book your refill on time, within 21 days.
