# Microshading

Microshading is a modern, semi-permanent eyebrow enhancement technique designed to create fuller, more defined brows with a soft, powdered finish. Unlike traditional microblading, which involves creating hair-like strokes, microshading uses a specialized machine to implant pigment into the upper layers of the skin using a gentle, stippling motion. The result is a gradient effect that mimics the look of perfectly applied brow makeup—subtle, sophisticated, and effortlessly polished

This service is ideal for individuals with sensitive or oily skin, sparse or patchy eyebrows, or those who prefer a more makeup-ready appearance. Whether you’re aiming to enhance your natural brows or correct asymmetry, microshading delivers long-lasting results that typically last 1 to 2 years with proper care.
